Bright Sparx Electrical & Security is family owned and operated, servicing Sydney and the greater metropolitan area. We handle residential and commercial work: installations, maintenance, CCTV, alarms, data cabling, lighting upgrades, switchboards and smart home systems.

Electricity is dangerous and unpredictable. Getting your property up to modern standards cuts the risk, improves efficiency and saves money over time. With over 30 years in the industry behind us, every job gets the same reliable workmanship and honest advice, whether it is a large commercial fitout or a single power point.
