01. Legal

Privacy policy

Last updated 28 August 2026.

Who we are

Bright Sparx Electrical & Security Services (ABN 56 633 675 626) is a family owned electrical and security contractor based in Prestons, NSW, servicing Sydney and the greater metropolitan area. This policy explains how we handle the personal information you give us through this website.

What we collect

When you fill in the quote form on this site, we collect:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • The suburb the job is in
  • The service, property type and timing you select
  • Anything you choose to write in the message field

We do not collect payment details through this website, and we do not ask for identity documents.

How we use it

We use your details for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry, quote the work and arrange a time. If you become a customer, we keep the same details on file so we can service your job and honour the workmanship guarantee.

We do not add you to a marketing list without asking, and we do not send bulk promotional email.

Storage and sharing

Enquiries submitted through this site are delivered to our business email and stored in our own records. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with third parties for marketing.

We may share limited details where it is needed to do the job or where the law requires it, for example with a supplier arranging a delivery to your address, or with your insurer if you have asked us to prepare an electrical damage report for a claim.

Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Standard web server logs may record your IP address and browser type as part of normal hosting operation.

Access, correction and deletion

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@brightsparxelectrical.com.au and we will action the request. We may need to keep some records where tax or electrical compliance law requires it.

Complaints

If you are not happy with how we have handled your information, contact us first on 0423 168 780 or by email and we will work it out. If you are still not satisfied, you can raise it with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle personal information, we will update this page and change the date at the top.
